Our Story

Edinburgh's Finest Bangladeshi Kitchen

Bangla Kitchen is a combination of subtle tastes with the emphasis on fresh ingredients and exotic spices, delicately blended to create the most spectacular and mouth-watering dishes.

Our chef has also used his culinary skills to produce some unique Bangladeshi dishes for your eating pleasure. We have been proudly serving Edinburgh for over a decade, winning multiple awards along the way.

We are a finalist for the Scottish Curry Awards 2026 — a testament to our commitment to quality and our wonderful customers who continue to support us.
